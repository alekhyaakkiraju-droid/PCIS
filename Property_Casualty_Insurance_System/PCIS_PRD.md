# PCIS Product Requirements Document

## User Personas
1. Claims Adjuster — processes FNOL and manages reserves
2. Claims Supervisor — approves payments exceeding authority limit
3. CSR — customer service representative
4. Billing Analyst — manages billing schedules
5. Premium Analyst — reviews premium calculations
6. Commission Manager — manages agent commissions
7. IT Operations — monitors batch jobs
8. Compliance Officer — reviews audit trails
9. Finance Analyst — reviews financial reports

## Feature Requirements
- FR-001: Customer 360 view with PII masking
- FR-002: Policy issuance with premium breakdown
- FR-003: Claims FNOL intake with 5-step wizard
- FR-004: Claim payment with authority approval workflow
- FR-005: Billing installment schedule display
- FR-006: Batch job monitoring console
- FR-007: Audit trail with masked old/new values
